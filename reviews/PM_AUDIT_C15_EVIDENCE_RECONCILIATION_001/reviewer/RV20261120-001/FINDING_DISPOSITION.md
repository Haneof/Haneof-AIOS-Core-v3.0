### NI-01 — R6 live-provider evidence is post-baseline and cannot attest model identity

The R6 receipt's backing files (`src/aios_core/runtime/grok_provider.py`, `tests/runtime/test_live_multimodel_habitation.py`) were introduced by `7d70e123c91de29c4605f916dec3e8e41917c28f` (2026-10-20), nine commits after the audit baseline, and do not exist at `259d0353`. The live test is skipped by default and was never part of the pinned audit inputs. Worse, `ModelCallProvenance.model` is the *requested* model string; the server-returned `model` field is never read (`grok_provider.py:745,939` read only `chunk.get("id")`), and one assertion (`"AIOS_ONLINE" in text or "online" in text.lower() or len(text.strip()) > 0`) is trivially true for any non-empty text. The receipt's "4 passed in 242.87s" is a pasted console transcript from a Windows host (`D:\手镯开发\…`), not a CI run; no raw SSE log, request-id binding, or persisted usage/provenance record is supplied. **Effect:** the R6 "VERIFIED" marking has no verifiable basis in the pinned audit scope and no identity-attestation basis at all. **Needs:** a live re-capture at a stated pinned commit, with the server `model` field, request-ids, timestamps, and usage persisted — and the provenance code fixed to read the server's model field before any such re-capture can prove R6.
