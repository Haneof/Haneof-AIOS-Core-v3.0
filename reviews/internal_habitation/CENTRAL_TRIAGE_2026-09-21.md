- the father was believed normal but a `2025-05-12` Observation later shows high blood pressure;

### Revision continuity contradiction

The first sampled trace entry shows:

- `world_revision_before: 803`
- `world_revision_after: 804`

The following day-1 entry then shows:

- `world_revision_before: 0`
- `world_revision_after: 3`

A single causal annual World cannot move backward from revision 804 to revision 0 without an explicitly separate World/run identity and corresponding proof. The trace is therefore not a trustworthy monotonic causal record of one Resident life.

### Capability-log inconsistency

A sampled Periodic Review entry lists a capability call such as `commit_operation_experience` while its own recorded `capabilities_available` list does not include that capability. This further weakens the trace as direct RuntimeSnapshot evidence.

### Conclusion

The 459-line JSONL is not sufficient proof of 459 real model calls. It is retained as an unverified synthetic trace/artifact until an independently replayable causal chain can be produced.

No Core fix should be accepted merely because a disqualified habitation report says the behavior is wrong.
