## 1. Executive ruling

C14 creates a durable opportunity for the **same Resident AI** to inspect newly organized world evidence and decide whether AI-world cognition should change.

The governing loop is:

```text
world facts
  -> Dimension Summary
  -> durable COGNITIVE_DERIVATION opportunity
  -> same Resident CognitiveRuntime
  -> search / inspect / compare / ALL_DIMENSIONS / counter-evidence
  -> leaf-grounded cognition OR silence
  -> later retrieval and observable decision use
  -> Outcome / new evidence
  -> revise / weaken / retract / retain
```

C14 never means:

```text
Summary -> Claim
```

It means:

```text
Summary -> "there may be something worth inspecting"
```

A Summary may trigger, navigate, compress, and contextualize cognition. A Summary may not become the terminal self-proof of durable high-level cognition.

## 2. Legal basis and governance decision

**No constitution or registry modification is required.**

The current law already supplies the controlling rules:

- Fused Baseline §2.4 separates Observation, Evidence, Claim, and Summary.
- Fused Baseline §2.5 gives high-level meaning and cross-dimensional interpretation to AI, not deterministic code.
- Fused Baseline §2.7 says Summary answers what happened, not what it means.
- Fused Baseline §2.8 makes ALL_DIMENSIONS an observation projection, not causal truth.
- Fused Baseline §§2.12–2.15 put AI cognition in the unified World and require evidence, revision, and forward evolution.
- Fused Baseline §2.16 permits durable non-user Wake to enter the same Resident CognitiveRuntime and defines Wake Reason as a task pointer rather than a conclusion.
- Dimension Summary Constitution §§2, 5, 7, 8 keep Summary temporal, source-traceable, and distinct from cognition.
- AI Dimension Constitution §§2, 4, 5, 6 requires durable cognition to be evidence-traceable, revisable, and not generated by fixed thresholds.
- AI Cognitive Runtime Constitution §§4, 5, 8, 10, 11 preserves active search/compare/project autonomy, evidence-grounded writeback, deterministic/semantic separation, and observable audit without private chain-of-thought.
- Periodic Review Constitution §§2, 3, 6, 7, 12, 14 already establishes same-Resident review, pinned anchors, no maintenance self-loop, and real-model semantic validation.

The unresolved item was an implementation-level interpretation of **sufficient support when Summary participates in C14 evidence**. That can be authoritatively resolved here without changing higher law.

## 3. Defined terms

### 3.1 Durable high-level cognition

For C14, durable high-level cognition means a C14-originated or C14-revised semantic AI-world object whose purpose is to express an understanding rather than merely record a raw fact or maintenance state.

This includes Claim and equivalent user-understanding, relationship, self, strategy, calibration, personality, cognitive-boundary, or other cognition-domain writeback using the existing Claim / EvidenceSet / Dependency / Revision machinery.

### 3.2 Support provenance closure

**Support provenance closure** is the recursively expanded, mechanically inspectable set of persisted support ancestors reachable from a proposed cognition's support EvidenceSet and support/dependency lineage until terminal non-container leaves are reached.

A conforming closure:

1. follows **exact pinned revisions**;
2. follows persisted `source_refs`, EvidenceSet support/member lineage when semantically used as support, and registered Dependency edges that express support/source lineage;
3. recursively expands Summary and other support containers;
4. preserves subject isolation and the existing explicit user + AI-self private-world exception only where already legal;
5. never substitutes an unpinned latest revision for the pinned source;
6. never uses Summary prose, Claim prose, keywords, event counts, model confidence, or semantic NLP to classify provenance;
7. detects missing refs, illegal cross-subject refs, cycles, unsupported edge types, corrupt lineage, or traversal failure;
8. treats any incomplete lineage needed to establish immediate eligibility as `UNKNOWN` and fails closed.

A Summary referenced by an EvidenceSet is therefore **not a terminal support leaf**. Its pinned source/dependency lineage must be expanded.

### 3.3 Qualifying non-Summary leaf-world evidence

A qualifying leaf is a durable **non-Summary** world object whose persisted provenance and object semantics are appropriate to the proposition being asserted.

For user/reality-facing cognition, normal qualifying leaves include:

- `SourceClass.USER` durable Observation or equivalent user-origin fact;
- `SourceClass.SENSOR` durable Observation or equivalent sensor fact;
- `SourceClass.PLATFORM` durable platform fact or validated execution result;
- real Outcome tied to the actual Action/capability execution it describes;
- directly captured Event/fact objects only when their pinned lineage resolves to qualifying USER / SENSOR / PLATFORM / applicable SAFETY provenance rather than to an unsupported cognition loop.

`SourceClass.SAFETY` is reality-facing for the safety/control fact it records, but its authority is **scope-limited**. SAFETY-only evidence does not by itself establish a user preference, personality, relationship meaning, desire, or general factual claim about the user.

The following are not terminal proof of user/world reality by themselves:

- Summary;
- EvidenceSet;
- Dependency;
- Claim / Revision / Retraction;
- Wake or Attention Bundle;
- scheduler/budget/review state;
- MeteringRecord or model usage;
- maintenance markers;
- AI-authored text merely because it was persisted;
- OperationExperience or CommunicationExperience whose own pinned case lineage does not reach the real case/feedback it summarizes.

A USER-sourced object proves at minimum that the user supplied or expressed the referenced content. It does not automatically convert every user assertion into independently verified external truth. The cognition's wording and knowledge state must preserve that distinction.

## 4. Summary legal status in C14

Summary may be used as:

- trigger anchor;
- navigation/compression;
- cognition context;
- review context;
- EvidenceSet `member_ref`;
- EvidenceSet `context_ref`;
- EvidenceSet `support_ref` or `counter_ref` **only when its transitive closure supplies the qualifying support/counter leaves required by the proposition**;
- a path to original Observation / Event / Outcome / other leaf evidence.

Summary-only support is insufficient.

Legal example:

```text
Claim
  -> EvidenceSet
      -> Week Summary
          -> Day Summary
              -> Observation@pinned-revision
```

Illegal self-closing example:

```text
assistant raw
  -> conversation Summary
  -> month Summary
  -> Claim
```

when no qualifying user/reality leaf establishes the claimed user fact.

Also illegal:

```text
AI Claim
  -> AI-dimension Summary
  -> new Claim
```

when the new Claim is only a restatement/amplification of prior AI cognition and no qualifying case evidence was added.

The rule is **not** "Claim may never cite Summary". The rule is:

> Summary may carry the Resident to evidence; Summary may not terminate the proof.

## 5. Support closure for new cognition, revision, weakening, and retraction

### 5.1 New durable cognition

A C14-created durable high-level cognition must pass support closure before commit.

For a user/reality-facing proposition, the closure must include at least one qualifying reality-facing non-Summary leaf, and all lineage required to establish that eligibility must be resolvable.

A larger number of Summary objects never substitutes for a qualifying leaf.

### 5.2 Revision or weakening

A replacement or weakened cognition is still a semantic assertion. Its new support/counter evidence must satisfy the same closure rule.

An old valid Claim cannot be revised merely because a new Summary text sounds different.

### 5.3 Retraction

Retraction is legal when supported by either:

- qualifying leaf counter-evidence / later Outcome / user-world correction; or
- a mechanically auditable invalidation of the prior support contract, such as discovery that required evidence was stale, corrupt, cross-subject, unresolvable, or otherwise never qualified.

Retraction may not be justified solely by a new Summary recursively summarizing the old Claim.

### 5.4 Counter-evidence

A Summary may point to counter-evidence, but counter-evidence that is used to change durable cognition must also drill down to an appropriate non-Summary leaf or mechanically auditable support invalidation.

## 6. AI-self / strategy / calibration cognition is legal without turning it into a user fact

C14 must distinguish **what the cognition is about**.

AI-self, strategy, calibration, and method cognition may legally use the AI's own real case evidence, including:

- actual Action and its real Outcome;
- capability execution and its durable result;
- CommunicationExperience that pins actual user/world feedback;
- OperationExperience that pins the real case it summarizes;
- prediction/evaluation records that pin what actually happened;
- prior AI cognition as context or dependency.

For these non-user propositions, an appropriate direct operational/case leaf can satisfy the non-Summary grounding requirement even when the object is about the AI's own behavior rather than the user's traits.

However:

1. a prior AI Claim is not, by itself, a new real case;
2. an AI Summary of AI cognition is not, by itself, a new real case;
3. OperationExperience / CommunicationExperience must be recursively inspected when the asserted conclusion depends on the underlying case;
4. AI-self evidence cannot be silently re-labeled as proof that the **user** prefers, believes, intends, feels, or is a certain kind of person;
5. if a self/strategy cognition makes a user-facing subclaim, that subclaim requires qualifying user/reality support.

This preserves legitimate AI learning from its own Outcomes without converting AI-authored material into user truth.

## 7. Derived lineage classification

C14 may expose a mechanically derived runtime view:

- `REALITY`
- `AI_COGNITION_ONLY`
- `MAINTENANCE_ONLY`
- `MIXED`
- `UNKNOWN`

This is **not** a new persisted semantic source truth. It is recomputed from the existing durable `SourceClass` associated with exact object revisions plus pinned source/dependency lineage.

### 7.1 Atomic source interpretation

For terminal, semantically relevant leaves:

- `USER`, `SENSOR`, `PLATFORM`, and applicable `SAFETY` -> reality-facing;
- `AI_COGNITION` -> AI-cognition;
- `MAINTENANCE` -> maintenance.

The classification of a **container** is not determined by the SourceClass of the operation that created that container.

In particular, current Dimension Summary commits are maintenance writes. Therefore:

> `Summary commit SourceClass == MAINTENANCE` does **not** imply that the Summary's content lineage is `MAINTENANCE_ONLY`.

The implementation must recursively follow the Summary's pinned `source_refs` and support Dependency lineage.

### 7.2 Aggregate classification

After recursive leaf expansion:

1. any unresolved required branch -> `UNKNOWN`;
2. at least one reality-facing leaf **and** at least one AI-cognition leaf -> `MIXED`;
3. reality-facing leaf/leaves and no AI-cognition leaf -> `REALITY` (maintenance scaffolding does not erase real provenance);
4. AI-cognition leaf/leaves and no reality-facing leaf -> `AI_COGNITION_ONLY` (maintenance scaffolding does not create reality);
5. maintenance leaves only -> `MAINTENANCE_ONLY`;
6. no resolvable terminal leaves -> `UNKNOWN`.

The runtime view may also expose mechanical counts/flags such as `has_reality`, `has_ai_cognition`, `has_maintenance`, leaf refs, and unresolved refs. These are audit data, not semantic scores.

### 7.3 Routing ruling

- `REALITY` -> may create an immediate C14 derivation opportunity.
- `MIXED` -> may create an immediate opportunity **only because it contains real lineage**; the trigger carries no conclusion, and AI-authored material is not promoted to independent user fact.
- `AI_COGNITION_ONLY` -> no immediate C14 self-derivation.
- `MAINTENANCE_ONLY` -> no semantic C14 derivation.
- `UNKNOWN` -> fail closed for immediate derivation; Periodic Review remains the backstop.

Only a current/active, complete-enough Summary revision may create the immediate opportunity. `STALE`, `PARTIAL`, `MISSING`, inactive, tombstoned, or otherwise non-current Summary state does not create a new immediate semantic opportunity. A later valid CURRENT revision may do so as a new revision.

### 7.4 Higher-scale Summary recursion

Higher-scale Summary is legal:

```text
Year
 -> Month
   -> Week
     -> Day
       -> Observation
```

provided every hop is pinned and mechanically resolvable.

The number of Summary levels must never change source truth. Summary depth is compression depth, not evidence strength.

## 8. Cross-dimensional cognition ruling

C14 must **not** be engineered as:

```text
dimension X Summary -> dimension X Claim
```

A Cognitive Derivation run must preserve Resident access to the normal world capabilities, including:

- search;
- inspect exact world objects;
- timeline;
- compare existing cognition;
- ALL_DIMENSIONS projection;
- evidence/original-observation drill-down;
- Outcome drill-down;
- counter-evidence search.

The Resident decides:

- whether one dimension is enough;
- whether multiple dimensions are relevant;
- which dimensions are relevant;
- whether the apparent pattern is causal, correlational, contextual, or unresolved;
- whether to form, revise, retract, weaken, or leave cognition unchanged.

Deterministic Core must not encode:

- required dimension count;
- a fixed A+B+C semantic mapping;
- a minimum number of dimensions for Claim creation;
- "N occurrences = preference/personality/strategy";
- keyword-to-cognition rules;
- semantic importance scores.

C14-RES-001 must include a real-model case where A alone, B alone, and C alone are insufficient, while the joint multi-week world supplies a legitimate cognition opportunity. The evaluator may judge that the Resident should remain uncertain; no hidden expected Claim text is permitted.

## 9. Silence is a first-class successful outcome

A COGNITIVE_DERIVATION Wake means only:

> inspect whether durable understanding should change.

The Resident may legally finish with no semantic writeback.

Valid silence/no-op must not create:

- a synthetic Claim;
- fake OperationExperience;
- fake Outcome;
- a Claim such as "the model chose silence, therefore the user dislikes reminders";
- a user-facing response solely because a background derivation model returned text.

The Wake may complete for audit/lifecycle purposes without inventing semantic evidence.

## 10. Cognition must be consumed by future behavior

C14 is not complete when a Claim is written.

The required semantic loop is:

```text
Cognition
 -> later normal retrieval
 -> observable decision influence
 -> Outcome / new evidence
 -> cognition update or evidence-grounded retention
```

C14-RES-001 must prove at least one case with all of the following:

1. the original model session ends;
2. the original Runtime instance is discarded;
3. a new Runtime is created;
4. a new `session_id` is used;
5. old chat context is not copied;
6. only durable AIOS World / Index and legal checkpoint state are restored;
7. a similar but non-identical new reality occurs;
8. the Resident recovers the current cognition through normal AIOS context/search capabilities;
9. that cognition materially participates in a new observable Goal / Task / Wake / Response / Strategy or equivalent decision;
10. a later Outcome / user-world fact / contradiction can cause revise, weaken, retract, or evidence-grounded retention.

No private chain-of-thought is required or stored.

Sufficient observable evidence includes world refs/revisions, retrieval/search/context records, capability calls, writeback refs, decision artifacts/output, Outcome refs, and later revision/retention evidence.

Merely showing that the Claim still exists in SQLite is **not** behavioral consumption.

## 11. Positive and matched-negative Resident evidence

C14-RES-001 must contain both.

### 11.1 Positive control

```text
sufficient real evidence
 -> Summary
 -> derivation opportunity
 -> Resident inspection
 -> leaf-grounded cognition
 -> later contradiction
 -> revise / weaken / retract / retain with evidence
 -> later behavior consumes current cognition
```

### 11.2 Matched negative control

```text
superficially similar repetition
 -> Summary
 -> derivation opportunity
 -> Resident actually inspects evidence
 -> evidence insufficient / causes differ / counter-evidence exists
 -> silence / UNKNOWN / no unsupported durable cognition
```

The positive and negative cases should have close or equal event counts where practical.

The test exists specifically to detect accidental:

- occurrence thresholds;
- Claim quotas;
- wake-to-claim targets;
- fixed preference/personality/strategy conversion rules.

PASS must never depend on Claim count, cognition conversion rate, Wake count, token count, or evaluator impressions that the model merely "looks smart".

## 12. Continuous Derivation vs Periodic Review

The mechanisms coexist.

### Continuous Derivation

Purpose:

- near-term opportunity after a current eligible Summary revision changes temporal structure;
- bounded BACKGROUND attention;
- inspect and optionally change cognition now;
- idempotent and recoverable;
- no semantic conclusion at trigger time.

### Periodic Review

Purpose:

- longer-window consolidation;
- contradiction review;
- calibration;
- strategy learning;
- unresolved/UNKNOWN revisit;
- recovery backstop for evidence that was not immediately eligible, was deferred, or required broader hindsight.

Continuous Derivation does not replace Periodic Review.

Periodic Review must not become an excuse to drop a valid durable C14 opportunity after crash/budget deferral.

## 13. Scheduler/runtime implementation obligations frozen by this ruling

### 13.1 C14-SCHED-001

The scheduler must:

- use a dedicated auditable `COGNITIVE_DERIVATION` Wake source unless a later implementation proves an existing source is exactly equivalent without semantic ambiguity;
- route it BACKGROUND by default;
- reuse WakeBus, AttentionRouter, existing background budget, and existing lifecycle;
- derive lineage mechanically as §7 defines;
- create exactly one opportunity for an eligible Summary revision;
- create a new opportunity for a later eligible Summary revision;
- not repeat unchanged Summary work;
- not immediately schedule AI_COGNITION_ONLY, MAINTENANCE_ONLY, UNKNOWN, or non-current Summary;
- recover the commit->crash->missing-Wake gap without a second scheduler database;
- preserve subject isolation.

No scheduler code may infer the cognition itself.

### 13.2 C14-RUNTIME-001

The runtime must:

- dispatch through the same Resident CognitiveRuntime;
- expose the exact pinned Summary revision, dimension, window, derived lineage view, relevant current AI-world cognition, and normal capability catalog;
- preserve cross-dimensional search/inspect/compare/ALL_DIMENSIONS/counter-evidence autonomy;
- enforce this ruling's support closure before C14 cognition create/revise/retract writeback;
- prevent Summary-only and AI-self recursive support from bypassing T28;
- keep valid silence as a zero-semantic-write result;
- keep background derivation behind the existing delivery boundary;
- continue C13 non-world metering as the economic truth.

If the shared cognition write/revision service is the only safe enforcement point, C14-RUNTIME may harden that shared path rather than adding a C14-only bypassable validator, provided existing valid cognition semantics remain compatible with this ruling.

### 13.3 C14-LOOP-001

The loop hardening must prove:

- AI Claim -> AI Summary does not cause immediate infinite self-derivation;
- nested Summary lineage still resolves to leaves;
- sibling Summary bursts are mechanically coalesced/budgeted without semantic ranking;
- defer/restart/crash does not lose opportunity;
- hard deny has a durable non-semantic disposition and never means "already cognized";
- Periodic Review still performs long-window work;
- a legal cognition survives Runtime/session replacement and is retrievable through normal AIOS paths.

## 15. C14-CLOSE failure conditions

C14-CLOSE-001 must fail if any of these is true:

1. a durable high-level C14 cognition can close support only through Summary / AI cognition / maintenance recursion;
2. nested Summary provenance cannot mechanically reach leaves;
3. UNKNOWN/incomplete lineage is treated as immediate eligibility;
4. pure AI cognition can immediately self-trigger another derivation loop;
5. mixed lineage silently promotes AI-authored material into a user fact;
6. no true cross-dimensional positive opportunity was exercised by a real Resident;
7. no matched negative Resident inspection ended in justified silence/UNKNOWN/no unsupported cognition;
8. no cognition was recovered after actual session + Runtime replacement and used in a later observable decision;
9. later Outcome/contradiction cannot revise, weaken, retract, or evidence-groundedly retain cognition;
10. deterministic Core uses N-occurrence rules, keywords, fixed dimension counts, Claim quotas, conversion targets, or pseudo-LLM habitation;
11. C14 introduces a second persisted source truth, cognition database, scheduler database, or hidden cognition runtime;
12. required regressions are not green on the accepted candidate/main evidence.

## 16. Final semantic contract

The C14 contract is:

> **Summary tells Resident where temporal structure changed. Provenance tells the system whether an immediate inspection opportunity is legally routable. The Resident decides what the world means. Durable cognition must remain traceable beyond Summary to appropriate non-Summary case evidence, must survive context replacement, must affect later behavior through normal AIOS retrieval, and must remain correctable by later reality.**

This ruling closes C14-RULE-001 without modifying constitution or registry authority.
